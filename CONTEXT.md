# Cara

Cara is a caregiver-focused landing page and product experience for simplifying structured home care.

## Language

**Cara**:
The product that helps caregivers coordinate and follow home care activities.
_Avoid_: Care app, caregiver dashboard

**Caregiver**:
A person responsible for providing care at home for someone who needs recurring support.
_Avoid_: User, operator, nurse

**Routine**:
A recurring set of caregiving activities grouped by time or situation, such as morning care or night care.
_Avoid_: Schedule, checklist

**Task**:
A specific caregiving action within a **Routine** that a **Caregiver** can complete.
_Avoid_: Todo, procedure item

**Medical Note**:
A care observation recorded by a **Caregiver** for future reference.
_Avoid_: Log, memo, report

**Learn Center**:
The product area where a **Caregiver** finds guidance for common care scenarios.
_Avoid_: Knowledge base, help center, library

## Example dialogue

Caregiver: “I’m starting the morning Routine.”
Domain expert: “Great. Complete each Task in that Routine, then record any unusual observation as a Medical Note.”
Caregiver: “If I’m unsure how to do a Task?”
Domain expert: “Open the Learn Center for guidance before continuing.”
